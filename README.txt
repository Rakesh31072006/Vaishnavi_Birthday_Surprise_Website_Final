VAISHNAVI BIRTHDAY SURPRISE WEBSITE
====================================

The 15 photos are arranged in the exact order you sent them.

Open in VS Code:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html with Live Server.
4. Click "Open Your Surprise 💌".

Features:
- 15-photo automatic slideshow
- 4 x 4 photo gallery (15 photos + 1 empty space)
- Romantic red + soft-pink theme
- Birthday message and relationship story
- Two audio players
- Selfie Pulla and Memories Never End
- Floating hearts
